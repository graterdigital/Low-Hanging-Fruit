## Standards applied

- Titles: describe the page, match the H1's meaning, one brand suffix, no keyword or city lists, no stale years. Where Google already uses the title (or merely drops the brand or truncates the display) nothing is proposed; where Google swapped in the H1, the H1 is proposed.
- Descriptions: unique, from the page's own text, one or two sentences answering 'what will I find here', top GSC query worked in only when the page answers it; no claims the page does not make. Templated bios are fine when the fields are real (Google: programmatic descriptions from page data are acceptable).
- 'Title too long' and 'Meta description too long' are ignored on purpose: Google states no length limit. The skill never truncates.
